# Design brief — numeri/que/ garden

> Written after the fact, from the codebase and content, during the design pass
> of 2026-09-11. Sections marked **(to confirm)** are inferences, not decisions
> the author stated.

## Context

**Audience** — technically literate readers arriving from a link: ML engineers,
data scientists, maths-adjacent people. They come for one long article, read it
in one sitting, and rarely browse. Reading is the whole job of this site.

**Tone (to confirm)** — *quiet, dense, literate*. Evidence: a strictly
monochrome palette, no marketing surface anywhere, long-form posts carrying
heavy KaTeX, and classical paintings as banners (Géricault for extreme values,
Renoir for distribution distance, Fragonard for post-training). The paintings
are the clearest signal of intent — they are chosen for meaning, not decoration.

**Theme** — both, honestly. A reader may open a maths-heavy post at any hour;
neither ground is the "real" one. Both are designed, not inverted.

## System

**Type** — Geist Variable, self-hosted (100–900 axis), with Geist Mono for code.
A deliberate choice, not a system default. Body at 16px / 1.75, lifted to 1.85
in dark mode. Running text is capped at 68ch; code, tables and display maths
break out of that measure on wide screens.

**Colour** — OKLCH throughout. Neutrals carry a trace of the brand hue (275)
rather than sitting at chroma 0. Two colour roles, kept strictly apart:

- `--accent` — a neutral hover/focus *surface*. Never a brand colour.
- `--brand` — the blue-violet, spent in exactly three places: the focus ring,
  the TOC current-section marker, and the stage dot. It is rare on purpose.

**Callouts** — 24 variants from one rule on two axes. Hue = family (six, 60°
apart). Intensity = weight within that family. Only the label takes the hue;
body text stays in the prose colour. See `_design/docs/callout-system.html`.

**Garden metadata** — optional `stage` (seedling / growing / evergreen) and
`updated` fields. A post without them behaves exactly as before.

**Banners** — optional `banner` object (artwork / artist / year / source),
rendered as a caption and used for image alt text.

## Conventions that look like mistakes

Do not "fix" these — they are deliberate, and confirmed by the author:

- French typography survives in English titles (the space before a colon).
- Raw, unrendered LaTeX inside model-completion blocks in the SFT/DPO/GRPO
  post: it is the literal model output, which is the point of the ablation.
- Top-level sections in posts are written as `#` (h1), not `##`. Tooling
  adapts to that — the TOC scroll-spy tracks `.prose h1`.
- No public email address in the footer.

## Known open items

- `--brand` sits at hue 275, close to the blue that `color.md` flags as an
  AI-design default. It predates this pass; changing it is a brand decision.
- Several images (Renoir banner, About page bento) are hotlinked from
  Wikimedia / Pinterest / Unsplash — outside the Astro image pipeline.
- The `callouts-component` draft documents the retired 25-variant palette and
  now contradicts the component.
